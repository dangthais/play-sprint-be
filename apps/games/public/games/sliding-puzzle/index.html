How to play

Slide the numbered tiles into order.

  • Goal: Arrange the tiles from 1 to 8 in order. The empty space should be in the bottom-right.
  • Move: Click or tap any tile that is next to the empty space (above, below, left, or right). That tile will slide into the empty spot.
  • Score: The fewer moves, the better.
